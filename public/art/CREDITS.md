# Campus artwork

The articulated student mesh and UV atlas are derived from the four approved v2 character turnarounds in `assets/characters/hero/`. The limb pivots are stored in the GLB nodes. The separate dye mask preserves the folds, face and lettering across outfit changes. Plant cutouts, planting geometry and architecture were authored for this scene.

The following Poly Haven assets are distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). All runtime maps are served locally.
